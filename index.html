Tot

Tot is an elegant, simple way to collect and edit text across your Mac, iPhone, and iPad. It’s your tiny text companion—minimal, powerful, always there.

Less Is More

Single window design with simple formatting controls. Seven color-coded dots keep your notes organized without clutter.

Dark and Light Mode

Stunning in any light. Fully embraces macOS and iOS themes for seamless day-to-night use.

Small Footprint

Minimal chrome on desktop, Menu bar control. On mobile, swipe to any dot. Stays out of your way.

iCloud Sync

Seamless sync across devices via iCloud. Capture thoughts anywhere, access everywhere.

Markdown and More

Rich text, plain text, RTF to Markdown conversion. Killer keyboard with word and character counts.

How It Works

1. Collect

Swipe or click a color-coded dot to start a note. Jot down ideas instantly across Mac, iPhone, iPad.

2. Edit

Simple controls for formatting. Markdown support converts RTF effortlessly. Keyboard shortcuts for speed.

3. Sync

iCloud handles the rest. Notes appear on all devices in real time, no effort required.

4. Access

Menu bar on Mac, swipe on mobile. Floating window option with Shift-Command-F (customizable).

FAQ

Does it work on macOS Tahoe?

Yes, with a partial workaround for popover bugs when Menu bar is hidden. Window sizing fixed.

Is there a purchase required?

No—this is the fully activated version of Tot 2.1.1. Ready to use immediately.

How do I customize shortcuts?

What about mobile?

Native iOS/iPadOS app. Swipe between dots, full sync with Mac.
